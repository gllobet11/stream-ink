# StreamInk – Privacy Policy

StreamInk does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.
All drawings live in the page's memory and disappear when the page is reloaded or closed.
The extension makes no network requests and uses no analytics or remote code.

Permissions used:
- `activeTab` / `scripting`: inject the drawing overlay into the tab you activate it on (toolbar click or `Alt+D`).

Contact: https://github.com/gllobet11/stream-ink/issues
